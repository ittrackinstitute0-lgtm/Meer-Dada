Heritage of Excellence

The Art of Meer Dada

Founded on the relentless pursuit of horological perfection, Meer Dada stands at the pinnacle of luxury watchmaking. Each timepiece requires hundreds of hours of painstaking hand-polishing, bevelling, and adjustment.

We do not simply tell time; we encapsulate legacy. From our hand-drawn blueprints to our hand-assembled flying tourbillons, every component is scrutinized under 20x magnification to ensure eternal durability.

  • Hand-bevelled Côtes de Genève stripes on bridges
  • Heat-tempered blued steel screws & escapement gears
  • Chronometer grade testing across 5 positions & 3 temperatures
